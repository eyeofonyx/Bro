Local changes

The upstream core binary and its Emscripten loader are unmodified. The upstream disc worker has two added messages, playerStatus and playerPause, exposing the existing core status and pause functions to this wrapper. The wrapper adds local ISO inspection, a checksummed core verifier, GameCube touch/keyboard input, a canvas player, audio controls, and lifecycle cleanup. Original source headers and notices are retained.

Experimental compatibility. Shipping an engine is not proof of Mario Kart Wii compatibility. Shared memory, cross-origin isolation, and roughly 1.5 GB of working memory are needed. No full Mario Kart Wii ISO was available to verify a game boot or race in this wrapper. iPhone / iPad gameplay is not verified. Source and software are supplied without warranty.
